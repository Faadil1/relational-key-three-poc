# Candidate A — CHILE / VALPARAÍSO FUNICULAR ASCENSORES

Working family:
**COUNTERWEIGHT MEMORY / OPPOSITE-MOTION REGISTER**

Place anchor:
Valparaíso, Chile

## Source grounding

Consejo de Monumentos Nacionales de Chile documents the historic Valparaíso `ascensores` as funicular transport created to connect the port/plain with hillside neighborhoods and protects multiple installations as historic monuments.

Technical/patrimonial documentation describes the characteristic funicular system as:
- two wooden or metal cars;
- moving simultaneously in opposite directions;
- one car functioning as counterweight to the other;
- shared traction/cable infrastructure.

Source anchors:
- https://www.monumentos.gob.cl/monumentos/monumentos-historicos/ascensores-de-valparaiso-artilleria-cordillera-hospital-van-buren
- https://www.monumentos.gob.cl/sites/default/files/articles-45668_doc_pdf.pdf
- technical heritage/seminar documentation on the Valparaíso funicular system.

## Native pair

**VERY HIGH.**

Card A — ascending car
Card B — descending car

They are distinct bounded members whose opposite motion is mechanically linked by the funicular system.

## Candidate law

# **ONE CAR'S OPPOSITE MOTION COUNTERBALANCES THE OTHER ON A SHARED CABLE.**

Potential signature:
`CAR A LOW ≠ CAR B HIGH → SHARED CABLE → A ASCENDS / B DESCENDS → CROSS → EXCHANGE HEIGHT → CONTINUE`

## Interaction translation

- direct vertical drag of either car;
- the paired car moves in the opposite direction at the same relational rate;
- neither car becomes a ghost follower — both remain equally visible;
- a central/shared cable line is support only, not the product;
- reversing one car reverses the other.

Alternate-valid state:
- choose a different starting height / direction; both remain valid;
- no artificial failure state required.

## Contribution

Primary topology:
# `R1 BALANCE / COUNTERWEIGHT`

Collection novelty: exceptional.

## Risks

- do not imply that the two cars alone power the system; modern/historic traction details vary and include external drive systems;
- frame the law as counterbalancing opposite motion on a shared funicular system, not perpetual gravity motion;
- avoid generic Valparaíso color/house iconography if not needed.

Provisional assessment:
**TOP-TIER / PROMOTION FAVORITE**

# Candidate B — ARGENTINA / URUGUAY REGION — MATE + BOMBILLA

Working family:
**FLOW MEMORY / FILTER REGISTER**

Primary context anchor for this round:
Argentina, with broader South American mate context acknowledged.

## Source grounding

Argentina's national cultural heritage inventory documents mate as yerba placed in a vessel, hot water added, and consumption through a `bombilla`; the practice is widespread and may be individual or shared socially.

The British Museum classifies bombillas explicitly as `mate-strainer` objects across South American collections.

Smithsonian NMAI holds documented bombilla / mate-straw objects from South American contexts.

Source anchors:
- https://www.argentina.gob.ar/cultura/manifestaciones-del-patrimonio-cultural-inmaterial/entre-rios/mate
- https://www.britishmuseum.org/collection/term/x7826
- https://www.si.edu/object/mate-strawbombilla%3ANMAI_207196

## Native pair

**HIGH / FUNCTIONALLY PAIR-NATIVE.**

Card A — mate vessel containing the particulate infusion field
Card B — bombilla / filtering straw

The material field belongs inside Card A rather than becoming a third hero object.

## Candidate law

# **A FILTERING STRAW TURNS A PARTICULATE INFUSION INTO SELECTIVE FLOW.**

Potential signature:
`MATE ≠ BOMBILLA → INSERT → SUBMERGE FILTER → DRAW → LIQUID PASSES / PARTICLES REMAIN → CONTINUE`

## Interaction translation

- insert/approach bombilla into the mate vessel;
- user applies a restrained `draw` gesture/pressure after pairing;
- liquid-flow marks travel through Card B while leaf particles remain in Card A;
- no literal mouth/person is represented.

Alternate-valid context:
- different documented mate preparations may remain valid if carefully sourced;
- first build can omit a mismatch entirely rather than invent one.

## Contribution

Primary topology:
# `R7 TRANSFER / SELECTIVE PASSAGE / CONTAINMENT`

Very distinct from Khipu, Siku, Astrolabe, Janney and Metate.

## Risks

- the social/ritual practice is broader than the filter subsystem; do not reduce the whole mate tradition to engineering;
- user suction/draw is an external action, so the UI must be explicit that it translates only the vessel↔bombilla functional subsystem;
- do not claim mate as exclusive to Argentina.

Provisional assessment:
**TOP-TIER / STRONG PAIR-NATIVE FINALIST**

# Candidate C — COLOMBIA / PASTO VARNISH MOPA-MOPA

Working family:
**LAYER MEMORY / CUT-REVEAL REGISTER**

Place anchor:
Putumayo + Nariño, Colombia

## Source grounding

UNESCO inscribed the traditional knowledge and techniques associated with Pasto Varnish mopa-mopa on the Intangible Cultural Heritage in Need of Urgent Safeguarding list in 2020. The element encompasses harvesting, woodwork and decorative varnishing, and safeguarding work is practitioner-led.

Historical Round 13K qualification also documented the process of resin preparation, pigmented thin sheets/layers, application and selective cutting/reveal.

Primary source:
- https://ich.unesco.org/en/USL/traditional-knowledge-and-techniques-associated-with-pasto-varnish-mopa-mopa-of-putumayo-and-narino-01599

## Native pair

**MEDIUM.**

Material layers are real, but splitting the documented craft into two independent cards is an editorial translation.

## Candidate law

# **A LAYER BECOMES INFORMATION THROUGH WHAT IT REVEALS BENEATH IT.**

Potential signature:
`UPPER LAYER ≠ LOWER FIELD → OVERLAY → SELECTIVE CUT → REVEAL → COMPOSITE`

## Contribution

Primary topology:
# `R3 FOLD / OVERLAP / LAYER REVEAL`

High novelty and strong visual potential.

## Risks

- pair architecture is less native than Funicular or Mate+Bombilla;
- practice is living heritage under urgent safeguarding;
- decorative pattern must never be invented and passed off as traditional;
- practitioner/community context raises the truthfulness threshold materially.

Provisional assessment:
**STRONG RESERVE / HIGH NOVELTY, HIGHER TRANSLATION BURDEN**

# Candidate E — COLOMBIA / EARLY QUIMBAYA — POPORO + PALILLO

Working family:
**ACCRETION MEMORY / CONTACT REGISTER**

## Source grounding

The Metropolitan Museum of Art documents Early Quimbaya poporos as lime containers. A `palillo` is inserted into the opening to scoop lime; the Met notes that repeated cleaning of the palillo can form a ring of lime/saliva around the opening.

Sources:
- https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/312954
- https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/312496

## Native pair

**HIGH.**

Card A — poporo
Card B — palillo

## Candidate law

# **REPEATED CONTACT LEAVES A MATERIAL MEMORY.**

Potential signature:
`DIP → WITHDRAW → CONTACT/WIPE → DEPOSIT → ACCRETE → TRACE OF USE`

## Contribution

Primary topology:
`R4 ACCUMULATION / USE-TRACE`, with unusual temporal memory.

## Risks — CURRENTLY DISQUALIFYING FOR PROMOTION

The museum evidence also ties poporo use to coca consumption and records significant social, ritual, sexual-symbolic and funerary meanings in different contexts, including living Indigenous traditions.

A portfolio interaction focused on residue/accretion risks aestheticizing body-fluid/coca/ritual context and flattening meanings that are not necessary for Continental Balance.

Provisional assessment:
# **HOLD / DO NOT PROMOTE IN THIS ROUND**

Mechanically excellent, culturally unnecessary risk given stronger alternatives.

# Candidate F — ECUADOR / BAHÍA — DOUBLE-CHAMBERED WHISTLING BOTTLE

Working family:
**FLOW-TO-VOICE / PRESSURE REGISTER**

## Source grounding

The Metropolitan Museum of Art documents Bahía double-chambered bottles whose chambers are connected. Hollow modeled figures act as whistles and produce sound as liquid is poured out. Related Vicús examples explain that liquid moving between connected chambers forces air through a whistle.

Sources:
- https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/316001
- https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/309386

## Native pair

**LOW-MEDIUM FOR RELATIONAL KEY.**

The two chambers are parts of one vessel rather than two autonomous counterparts.

## Candidate law

# **TRANSFER BETWEEN CONNECTED CHAMBERS TURNS LIQUID MOTION INTO AIRFLOW AND VOICE.**

Potential signature:
`CHAMBER A → LIQUID TRANSFER → CHAMBER B → AIR DISPLACEMENT → WHISTLE`

## Contribution

Primary topology:
`R7 transfer + pressure`, with sound as emergent output.

## Risks

- pair is structurally internal to one object;
- overlaps sound-medium neighborhood already represented by Ombak/Siku;
- weaker pair invariant than Funicular or Mate;
- sculptural figures and mythic imagery should not be decorative source material without need.

Provisional assessment:
# **RESERVE / DO NOT PROMOTE UNLESS STRONGER PAIR-NATIVE OPTIONS FAIL**
