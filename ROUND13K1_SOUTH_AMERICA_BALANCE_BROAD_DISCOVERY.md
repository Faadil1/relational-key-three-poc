## Candidate field

### A — COLOMBIA / PASTO VARNISH MOPA-MOPA
Potential family: **LAYER MEMORY / CUT-REVEAL REGISTER**

Grounding:
- UNESCO lists the traditional knowledge and techniques associated with Pasto Varnish mopa-mopa of Putumayo and Nariño.
- Museo Colonial describes the resin being cleaned/heated, pigmented, stretched into thin sheets, adhered to an object, then cut; multiple color layers form the final decoration.

Relational extraction:
`HEAT → STRETCH → LAYER → CUT → REVEAL → REGISTER`

Candidate law:
**A LAYER BECOMES INFORMATION THROUGH WHAT IT REVEALS BENEATH IT.**

Why it matters:
- materially distinct from Khipu;
- visual transformation occurs inside the material, not through an external connector;
- supports a clean two-layer interaction with reveal as emergent proof.

Risk:
- the historical craft is applied to a single decorated object, so the two-card translation must avoid pretending that artisans historically used two separate cards.

Sources:
- https://ich.unesco.org/en/USL/traditional-knowledge-and-techniques-associated-with-pasto-varnish-mopa-mopa-of-putumayo-and-narino-01599
- https://www.museocolonial.gov.co/colecciones/piezas-del-mes/Paginas/Mujer-con-falda-azul.aspx

### B — BRAZIL / PERNAMBUCO / COBOGÓ
Potential family: **SHADOW MEMORY / PERFORATION REGISTER**

Grounding:
- Brazilian institutional and academic sources trace the cobogó to Recife/Pernambuco in 1929.
- Its perforated geometry admits natural light and ventilation while providing partial privacy; sunlight passing through the openings produces changing shadow patterns.

Relational extraction:
`SCREEN → INCIDENT LIGHT → FILTER → PROJECT → SHADOW → REGISTER`

Candidate law:
**PERFORATION TURNS LIGHT INTO SPATIAL INFORMATION.**

Why it matters:
- exceptionally visual;
- introduces occlusion/projection rather than knot, fit, disparity, or beat;
- shadow can emerge only from screen × light relation.

Risk:
- one side of the pair is environmental rather than a conventional physical counterpart;
- must remain distinct from Swell Register by centering projection/occlusion, not environment-response generally.

Sources:
- https://www.camara.leg.br/tv/brasilidade/408106-cobogo/
- https://mam.rio/historia/cobogos-do-mam-rio/
- https://bdta.abcd.usp.br/item/003002004

Provisional score: **33/35**

### C — BOLIVIA / AYMARA SIKU / IRA + ARKA
Potential family: **DISTRIBUTED MELODY / COMPLEMENT REGISTER**

Grounding:
- Smithsonian Folkways documents Bolivian siku traditions.
- Smithsonian describes two complementary single-row pipes played by two individuals in alternating/hocketing fashion to produce a single scale and melody.
- Universidad de Chile MAPA describes arka and ira as dual/complementary halves whose alternating sounds weave one melody.

Relational extraction:
`NOTE A → ABSENCE → NOTE B → ALTERNATE → SCALE → MELODY`

Candidate law:
**COMPLEMENTARY ABSENCE PRODUCES CONTINUITY.**

Proof state:
`IRA ≠ ARKA → ALTERNATION → ONE MELODY`

Why it matters:
- probably the strongest literal pair-native mechanism in the field;
- neither half contains the full scale;
- the relation creates information unavailable to either instrument alone.

Risk:
- another sound-based family after Ombak;
- must prove its law through alternation/distributed notes, never frequency beating or resonance.

Sources:
- https://folkways.si.edu/news-and-press/unesco-collection-week-46-music-and-community-lifeways
- https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/504566
- https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/504565
- https://instrumentosmapa.uchile.cl/siku-sanka-ira-3866/

Provisional score: **32/35**

### D — ARGENTINA / URUGUAY / MATE + BOMBILLA
Potential family: **FLOW MEMORY / FILTER REGISTER**

Grounding:
- Argentina's cultural heritage register documents mate as yerba in a vessel, hot water, and consumption through a bombilla.
- Uruguay cultural-heritage material identifies the mate vessel, bombilla and related objects as a distinctive material/inmaterial practice.

Relational extraction:
`INFUSION → PARTICULATE FIELD → FILTER → SELECTIVE PASSAGE → FLOW`

Candidate law:
**SELECTIVE PASSAGE PRODUCES A CLEAN CONTINUATION.**

Why it matters:
- two immediately legible physical objects;
- introduces filtration/selection rather than alignment;
- very strong everyday cultural recognizability.

Risk:
- heritage sources strongly ground the practice but deeper technical sourcing is still required before making precise claims about filter geometry or flow behavior.

Sources:

### E — COLOMBIA / QUIMBAYA POPORO + PALILLO
Potential family: **ACCRETION MEMORY / CONTACT REGISTER**

Grounding:
- The Metropolitan Museum of Art documents Early Quimbaya poporos from Colombia.
- A palillo is inserted through the opening to scoop lime.
- The Met explicitly notes that a ring of lime and saliva may form around the opening as the palillo is cleaned.

Relational extraction:
`DIP → CONTACT → WITHDRAW → WIPE → DEPOSIT → ACCRETE → MEMORY`

Candidate law:
**REPEATED CONTACT LEAVES A MATERIAL MEMORY.**

Why it matters:
- extremely strong conceptual fit for RELATIONAL KEY;
- relation does not merely resolve once: its history accumulates physically;
- introduces temporal accretion as a new class of relationship.

Risk:
- high cultural-sensitivity burden because the documented practice is tied to coca use and can include specific social/ritual meanings;
- body-fluid residue should not be aestheticized or sensationalized;
- would require exceptionally careful truthfulness boundaries.

Sources:
- https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/312954
- https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/312496

Provisional score: **31/35**

### F — ECUADOR / BAHÍA DOUBLE-CHAMBERED WHISTLING BOTTLE
Potential family: **FLOW-TO-VOICE / PRESSURE REGISTER**

Grounding:
- The Met documents Bahía double-chambered bottles from coastal Ecuador.
- The two chambers are connected; the modeled hollow figures function as whistles and sound when liquid is poured out.
- Related Andean examples document liquid transfer forcing air through a whistle to produce sound.

Relational extraction:
`LIQUID → TRANSFER → PRESSURE → AIRFLOW → WHISTLE`

Candidate law:
**TRANSFER BETWEEN CHAMBERS PRODUCES VOICE.**

Why it matters:
- fluid mechanics creates an emergent outcome;
- highly sculptural and visually distinctive;
- another example of A + B producing C.

Risk:
- the two chambers belong to one vessel rather than two separate relational objects;
- sound output overlaps the collection's existing Sound Memory category even though the causal mechanism differs.

Sources:
- https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/316001
- https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/309387

Provisional score: **30/35**
