# 2. Source grounding

## Primary museum anchor — Museo Nacional de Antropología / INAH

`Metate zapoteco`, Pieza del Mes, June 2021:
https://mna.inah.gob.mx/detalle_pieza_mes.php?id=252

The museum text establishes that:
- a metate should be discussed together with the `metlapil`, described as its complement;
- both are made from the same volcanic-stone material in the documented object context;
- the metlapil is cylindrical/elongated;
- the names are explained through an expression interpreted by the museum as approximately `two that grind something`;
- metates have been used for food preparation, pigments and medicinal substances;
- the selected object is connected to Zapotec community context in Teotitlán del Valle, Valles Centrales de Oaxaca;
- contemporary uses persist, including grain/spice and specific food preparations.

## INAH mechanism corroboration

INAH glossary documentation defines:
- `metate` as the grinding surface used with a metlapil for maize, cacao and other grains;
- `metlapil` as the roller/grinding member used upon the metate.

Source:
https://www.voceszapatistas.inah.gob.mx/glosario/M

INAH technical/historical research describes Mesoamerican grinding as friction between elements against a resistant surface and documents the metate/metlapil technological complex across pre-Hispanic and contemporary contexts.

Additional source anchors:
- https://mediateca.inah.gob.mx/islandora_74/islandora/object/fotografia%3A289696
- INAH publication `El maíz prehispánico / ¿Qué sabemos de los instrumentos de molienda?`
- INAH historical museum annals defining `metate` and `metlapil` as complementary grinding implements.

# 5. Interaction contract

## Initial state — OFFSET

- two bounded cards are separate;
- metate already contains a small coarse material field;
- metlapil is visibly independent;
- no grinding progress is credited.

Label concept:
`METATE ≠ METLAPIL · NO ACTIVE GRINDING RELATION`

## Pair / Engage

Action:
`PAIR STONES`

Transition:
`OFFSET → APPROACH → CONTACT`

The cards approach only enough to make the working relation legible.

No automatic grinding occurs during pairing.

## Direct reciprocal stroke

After contact:
- user drags the metlapil directly across a horizontal working lane;
- motion may proceed left→right or right→left;
- a meaningful completed direction reversal counts as reciprocal work;
- tiny pointer jitter must not inflate progress;
- progression should depend on repeated passes, not elapsed time.

Preferred implementation model:
- track x-position continuously;
- detect directional travel beyond a minimum distance threshold;
- count a pass when travel reaches the opposite side / sufficient range;
- accumulate grind state per completed pass;
- clamp total progression to a finite visual range.

## Material transformation feedback

The material field on/within Card A may progressively change:
- coarse particles → medium → fine;
- particle size/distribution may become denser/finer;
- feedback remains local to the metate card.

Do not create a central pile/product as the reward.

## Continue

At a visually sufficient fine state, user may continue strokes.

The interaction law is continuous:
`MORE RELATIONAL WORK → MORE GRINDING`

There is no magical final success burst.

## Reset

`RESET`:
- separates/returns cards to initial relation;
- restores coarse material;
- resets stroke/grind progression;
- keeps both objects valid.
