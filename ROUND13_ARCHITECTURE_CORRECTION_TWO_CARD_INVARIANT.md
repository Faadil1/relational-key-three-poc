# ROUND 13 — ARCHITECTURE CORRECTION

## Canonical correction
The original RELATIONAL KEY signature remains the product foundation:

**TWO DISTINCT RELATIONAL OBJECTS → APPROACH → CONNECTION / RESOLUTION → CONTINUATION**

The Cultural Editions drifted toward single-card experiences. That is now explicitly rejected as the default architecture.

## Product invariant
Every Cultural Edition must preserve two distinct objects:

1. **Credential / Member Card** — carries the member claim.
2. **Counterpart / Destination Card** — carries the destination rule, context, service, place, archive, institution, or other relevant counterpart.

The relationship is not decorative. It is the core interaction.

Canonical model:

**CREDENTIAL × COUNTERPART → RELATION → ACCESS / EXPLANATION**

Canonical interaction lineage:

**ENCOUNTER → APPROACH → COMPARE → REGISTER / RESIST → CONNECT → CONTINUE → EXPLAIN**

## What variants may change
Cultural variants may transform:
- surface material
- photographic content
- color system
- typography
- physical metaphor
- local registration behavior
- connection behavior
- reveal behavior
- counterpart identity

But they may NOT remove:
- the presence of two distinct relational objects
- the spatial/visual gap between them before resolution
- the relationship event between them
- the continuation or resistance outcome

## Example translations

### Cultural Figure / Frida
- Card A: member credential / personal trace claim
- Card B: archive/context counterpart (Coyoacán / Casa Azul / trace archive)
- Connection: trace alignment / memory seam

### Food Memory / Toyama
- Card A: member credential / regional food-memory claim
- Card B: destination/food-context card representing Toyama / Masu no Sushi access context
- Connection: pressure-release relationship between cards; food reveal remains inside the card surfaces, not as a standalone product object

### Textile Memory / Asante-Bonwire
- Card A: credential with one woven strip family
- Card B: counterpart card with the corresponding strip/join context
- Connection: misregistered woven strips align across the inter-card gap, then join and continue

## Canonical visual law
**TWO CARDS FIRST → CULTURAL MATERIAL INSIDE → RELATIONSHIP BETWEEN → CONTINUATION AFTER**

## Status
This correction supersedes the single-card interpretation of:
`THE CREDENTIAL REMAINS THE OBJECT`

New stronger rule:
**THE RELATIONAL PAIR REMAINS THE PRODUCT.**

Single-card studies remain useful as material experiments, but are not the canonical end-state for Cultural Editions.
