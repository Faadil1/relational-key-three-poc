# SOURCE ANCHORS

## Te Papa — Model Tongiaki (sailing canoe)
`https://collections.tepapa.govt.nz/object/648909`

Te Papa identifies an eighteenth-century Tongan tongiaki model as a double-hulled sailing canoe with **two hulls of equal length joined together by a deck** supporting sail and rigging. The model materials include wood and fibre.

## Te Ara — Tongiaki in Tongatapu, Tonga
`https://teara.govt.nz/en/artwork/2213/tongiaki-in-tongatapu-tonga`

Te Ara identifies tongiaki as Tongan ocean-going double-hulled canoes documented at Tongatapu in 1643.

## Te Ara — Pacific origins / double hull
`https://teara.govt.nz/en/waka-canoes/page-1`

Te Ara documents the wider Polynesian structural principle that **two hulls were lashed together to form double-hulled vessels** capable of carrying people and cargo.

## Te Ara — Double-hulled canoe, Tonga, 1790s
`https://teara.govt.nz/en/artwork/4257/double-hulled-canoe-tonga-1790s`

The documented Tongan double hull has a **platform joining the two hulls with a wooden frame**.
