Why this relationship works

Masu-zushi is a Toyama pressed sushi made with trout, vinegared rice, bamboo leaves and a bentwood container. RELATIONAL KEY translates the real preparation logic — wrapping and pressure — into a conceptual registration/release interaction.

  • Real food and material photographs remain the visible cultural source.
  • The pressure register and claim system are conceptual interface mechanics.
  • No producer, museum or rail affiliation is claimed.
