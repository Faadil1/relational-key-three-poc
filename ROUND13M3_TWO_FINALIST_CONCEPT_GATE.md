## Why Boulle wins

### 1. The pair remains the product

Reticello creates a powerful third network between two directional layers. Boulle goes further for RELATIONAL KEY: the single relation event creates **two reciprocal finished outcomes**. Neither side becomes secondary after registration.

The proof is not merely:
`A + B → C`

It is:
`A + B → A' ↔ B'`

The relation transforms both objects while preserving their distinct identities.

### 2. Reciprocity is native, not editorial

The Met documents that stacked sheets are cut together and then separated so the resulting pieces can form première partie and contre partie. Historical paired furniture could therefore carry exact inverse material arrangements.

The reciprocal inversion is not an interface metaphor invented after the fact; it is inherent to the documented fabrication logic.

### 3. Strongest removal test

Remove either material sheet and the selected meaning disappears:
- there is no reciprocal set;
- no inverse receiving field;
- no partie ↔ contrepartie relationship.

A single sheet may still be cut, but the Boulle reciprocal pair is gone.
