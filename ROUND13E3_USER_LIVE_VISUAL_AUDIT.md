## What does not yet pass
The signature relationship event still reads as UI added between the cards rather than tessellation emerging from the cards themselves.

Observed weaknesses:
1. Matching produces a row of floating rhombi / central register elements in the gap, but the actual tile fields on the inner edges do not materially reconfigure.
2. The central register behaves like a glowing connector/control instead of grout / edge geometry.
3. The edge pieces on Card A and Card B do not visibly rotate, translate, interlock or close a joint.
4. The same source photograph is presented in a near-mirrored/sibling composition on both cards, so the pair can feel duplicated rather than complementary.
5. The final registered state does not yet create one unmistakable continuous tessellation spanning Card A → gap → Card B.
6. The no-match tilt is readable, but its causal relationship to the edge geometry is weak because the card edges themselves remain almost unchanged.

## Corrective direction
ROUND 13E.3.1 — EDGE MATERIAL REGISTRATION

Do not redesign the overall aesthetic. Keep the existing real-source material and two-card composition.

Replace the floating central-UI emphasis with a physical edge event:
- 3–5 larger cut-tile pieces originate visibly from the inner edge of each card.
- At APPROACH, opposing pieces expose their cut profiles.
- At ROTATE, actual edge pieces rotate by small, different angles.
- At FIT, Card A and Card B edge pieces translate toward complementary positions.
- At REGISTER, the grout channel narrows and the central register becomes subordinate or disappears.
- At TESSELLATE, a continuous geometric band spans both card surfaces and the gap.
- At CONTINUE, the completed band remains as the proof state.

No-match:
- pieces attempt alignment,
- one or more retain a visible angular/spacing residual,
- grout channel remains open,
- cards recoil slightly,
- both credentials remain valid.
