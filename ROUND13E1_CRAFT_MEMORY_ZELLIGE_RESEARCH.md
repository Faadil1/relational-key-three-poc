## Visual rules
1. Real source first: public-domain Moroccan tile photography is the material anchor.
2. The design must read as two CR80 credentials before registration.
3. Both card edges carry incomplete geometry.
4. The gap is not a generic glowing cable; it is a grout / edge-fit condition.
5. Compatible geometry rotates/fits and allows the tessellation to continue across both cards.
6. No-match leaves a visible geometric residual; neither credential is invalidated.
7. Do not claim the prototype reconstructs an exact historic zellige panel.
8. Do not use generic Moroccan tourism symbols, flag colors, lanterns, arches, or decorative Arabic typography as shortcuts.
9. Do not invent symbolic meanings for individual shapes/colors.
10. The edition should survive a monochrome geometry test.
