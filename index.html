SOURCE NOTES

Smithsonian National Museum of Asian Art documents ombak as frequency beating and identifies pengisup as the higher member and pengumbang as the lower member of a tuned pair. It notes common Bali practice of maintaining a shared ombak rate, with gong kebyar often around 7 Hz.
