Why this relationship works

Masu-zushi is a Toyama pressed sushi made with trout, vinegared rice, bamboo leaves and a bentwood container. The real food object is portable because wrapping and pressure hold the meal together. RELATIONAL KEY translates that physical logic into the interface: a compatible relationship releases the pressure and reveals the meal.
