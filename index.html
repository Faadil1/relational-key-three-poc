Why this relationship works

Masu-zushi already contains its own relational grammar: radial bamboo leaves, a circular bentwood container, compression, release, and reveal.

FOOD OBJECT
Trout and vinegared rice are layered in a round container lined with bamboo leaves and pressed.
RELATIONAL TRANSLATION
Pressure is treated as the latent state. A compatible claim releases that pressure, allowing the leaf structure to unfold and reveal the meal.
TRUTHFULNESS
This is a conceptual RELATIONAL KEY interface. The registration mechanism is fictional; the food, regional context, ingredients, and photography are real sources.
