Archive & truthfulness

The Smithsonian Archives of American Art documents Emmy Lou Packard’s 1941 photograph of Frida Kahlo’s studio and identifies it as U.S. public domain. The Museo Frida Kahlo describes Casa Azul and Frida’s personal objects as central to understanding her creativity and legacy. This prototype does not reproduce a Frida painting or claim museum affiliation.
