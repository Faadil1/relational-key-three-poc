## Source anchors

### National Gallery of Australia — Garamut drum, East Sepik Province / Ramu River
Documents a large garamut carved from a tree trunk and hollowed through the narrow slit along the top to create the resonating chamber. It is pounded with blunt sticks. Complex rhythms can function as codified communication over long distances between villages.

Source:
`https://digital.nga.gov.au/archive/exhibition/mythmagic/default.cfm?irn=211455&bioartistirn=211455&mystartrow=61&realstartrow=61&mnuid=3&galid=0&viewid=2`
