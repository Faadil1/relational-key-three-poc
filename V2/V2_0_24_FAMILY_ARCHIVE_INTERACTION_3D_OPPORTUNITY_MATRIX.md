Status: **MATRIX 001 / BUILD NOT AUTHORIZED YET**

## 0. Decision rule

V2 is not a visual refresh.

Every proposed family must satisfy both:

- **Relational Specificity Test** — remove either pair member and the memorable moment breaks.
- **Archive Specificity Test** — replace the archive rationale with an unrelated corpus and the interaction concept materially breaks.

No row below is a final historical or cultural claim. `Archive action` defines the research/provenance work required **before** a V2 implementation can promote.

## 1. Collision-first map

The 24 families intentionally share some primitive interaction classes. V2 must not allow those shared primitives to flatten family identity.

| Collision cluster | Families | Shared primitive | Differentiation requirement |
|---|---|---|---|
| Alignment / registration | City, Zellige, Kento, Astrolabe, Stereoscopy | relative positioning | consequence must differ: route continuation, material fit, transfer, celestial reading, depth emergence |
| Contact / material transformation | Coupler, Metate, Hika Ahi, Music Box | contact force | latch/load, abrasion, friction/heat, pin-to-comb decode must remain mechanically distinct |
| Wave / signal / temporal coupling | Signal Nigeria, Ombak, Swell Marshall, Siku, Garamut | propagation over time | relay, beat resonance, environmental deflection, complementary melody, ordered impact signal must not share one generic particle-wave effect |
| Tension / structural coupling | Khipu, Textile, Tongiaki | tension / connection | knot registration, interlacing continuity, hull stability must produce distinct physical consequences |
| Reveal / flow | Food Toyama, Mate + Bombilla | hidden/filtered transformation | unfolding/reveal and selective filtration must remain categorically different |
| Reflection / trace | Frida, Anamorphosis | optical relation | trace-to-register and optical rectification must not collapse into generic mirror effects |

Shared shaders, controls or physics utilities are allowed. Shared **memorable moments** are not.

Build a corpus of cylindrical/catoptric anamorphosis objects, diagrams and optical relationships from museum/scientific/print sources. Use rights-safe or purpose-built distorted imagery while preserving the documented optical mechanism.

**V2 interaction**  
A true 3D cylindrical mirror and anamorphic plane. User drags/rotates the cylinder relative to the distorted member; the reflected image in the mirror becomes legible only at the correct relation.

**Pair causality**  
Distorted field A → curved reflector relation → rectified reflection B.

**MATCHING**  
The reflection resolves into a legible registered form.

**OTHER**  
Both objects remain valid but the reflection stretches/breaks and refuses legibility.

**Memorable moment**  
An image that looks meaningless on one member becomes coherent only inside the other member's curved reflection.

**Archive specificity hook**  
The transformation must be genuine catoptric remapping; a simple texture crossfade fails.

**Accessibility alternative**  
Mirror position/rotation sliders, `step` controls, side-by-side distortion/rectification explanation, reduced-motion static checkpoints.

**Runtime risk**  
MEDIUM–HIGH. Reflection render target/shader must be profiled and gracefully downgraded.

**Priority**  
**PILOT 1 — optical/spatial pilot.**

## 24 — MUSIC BOX SAINTE-CROIX — Pin-to-Comb Register

**V1 mechanism**  
Stored pin geometry / contact / tuned-comb decode.

**Archive action**  
Build a Sainte-Croix/mechanical-music corpus for cylinder/pin/comb geometry, contact sequence, materials and industrial context. Audio reproduction must be rights-safe; generated tones can demonstrate mechanics without copying a protected recording.

**V2 interaction**  
Member A is the pinned cylinder; Member B is the tuned comb. User rotates/drags the cylinder. Pins physically cross the relation and deflect corresponding comb teeth; each tooth visibly vibrates and optionally sounds.

**Pair causality**  
Stored pin position A → contact relation → tuned tooth response B.

**MATCHING**  
Ordered pin contacts generate the expected bounded note/event sequence.

**OTHER**  
Cylinder/comb remain valid but offset alignment creates missed or incorrect contacts.

**Memorable moment**  
Geometry becomes time: fixed pins turn into ordered vibrations only when they physically meet the comb.

**Archive specificity hook**  
Pin/comb decode must be mechanically credible and corpus-derived; a generic piano-roll animation fails.

**Accessibility alternative**  
Rotation step buttons, event/teeth timeline, captions for notes, optional sound, reduced-motion terminal state.

**Runtime risk**  
MEDIUM–HIGH. Many collision/audio events; use deterministic event schedule rather than unrestricted physics.

**Priority**  
WAVE 2 — excellent post-pilot mechanism.
