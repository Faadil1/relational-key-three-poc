# City · Gatineau — collectible pair prototype

User intent: desirable, credible cards inspired by their experience of the STO Multi Jeunesse card; archival/documentary grounding; the pair remains the product. User approved the bounded City pilot.

## Sources and boundaries

The V1 City r5-8 family depicts west/east urban fragments and a conceptual river seam. This is the primary interaction reference, not a reconstruction of STO validation hardware.

Verified official references on 2026-09-08:
- Ville de Gatineau: https://www.gatineau.ca/ — city on both sides of the Gatineau river.
- Municipal sector map: https://www.gatineau.ca/portail/default.aspx?p=publications_cartes_statistiques_donnees_ouvertes%2Fcartes%2Fcarte_interactive_secteurs
- User's object reference: https://www.sto.ca/en/fares/multi-jeunesse/

These are contemporary documentary sources, not digitized historical archival images. No historical image, official branding or operational route is reproduced. Card diagrams are original editorial compositions; serials 001/A and 001/B identify the study, not a fare account or manufactured edition. Continent is location metadata, not an automatic compatibility rule.

## Delivered design

Landscape pocket-card proportions; rounded geometry and thin beveled edge; contrasting ink/paper palettes; large editorial titles; paired route diagrams; small selective iridescent strip. Canvas-generated typography is original artwork, not image generation or a document facsimile. Front/back switching is immediate, with the full verso text and municipal source link also accessible in the DOM.

Desktop presents side-by-side cards, mobile stacks them and redraws route endpoints to meet along the corresponding edge. Drag B toward A or use the native approach button. Registration prepares the seam; an explicit passage action activates the continuation. Showing either verso, separating the cards or choosing an offset relation prevents transfer. No animation timer; reduced-motion does not change the mechanism.

## Acceptance

Model tests cover contact, both rectos, explicit send, offset refusal, separation, reset and invalid input. City browser workflow verifies direct desktop drag, mobile tap, keyboard completion, source access, reset, reduced-motion idle, no browser errors and no horizontal overflow. Visual acceptance remains separate from automated passes.

This is an interactive digital collectible prototype, not a usable transit pass, print-production specification, authentic historical card or promise of virality. The user should assess whether the cards are desirable before expanding the treatment across the collection. City remains HUMAN HOLD until visual and documentary review.
