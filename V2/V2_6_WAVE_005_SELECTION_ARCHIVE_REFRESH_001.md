# Collision-first decision

## Selected — Metate · Teotitlán

Collision cluster: contact / material transformation.

Potential collisions already represented in V2:
- Coupler = contact → latch/load transfer
- Hika Ahi = friction → localized ember
- Music Box = pin contact → comb decode

Metate remains materially distinct because its consequence is **accumulated abrasion / fineness change caused by repeated reciprocal work**. It must not become a generic contact animation or particle grinder.

### Pair law

`METATE / RECEIVING GRINDING SURFACE → RECIPROCAL ABRASION / SHEAR → METLAPIL / MOVING GRINDING STONE + FINENESS RESPONSE`

### Memorable moment

Repeated reciprocal motion visibly changes the material field on the receiving stone; the finer lane cannot exist without both stones repeatedly working against each other.

### OTHER

Off-axis / edge-biased abrasion leaves a coarse ridge while both stones remain valid.

### Archive refresh

Primary retained anchors:
- Museo Nacional de Antropología / INAH documentation for the metate in Teotitlán del Valle
- NMAI Oaxaca metate + mano object evidence
- existing frozen R5.3 source/material-motion audit

The archive supports a paired lower grinding surface + upper moving stone and a Teotitlán-specific domestic/material context. V2 must derive specificity from object/use mechanics, not generic Mexican or Zapotec decoration.

### Rights / asset policy

`PROCEDURAL_ONLY_FOR_BUILD__INAH_AND_NMAI_REFERENCE_TEXT_OBJECT_METADATA_ONLY`

No archive raster is required for the concept build. A reusable documentary image may be considered later only if rights and product need are both explicit.

### Truth boundary

`Editorial visualization of documented Teotitlán/Oaxaca metate + metlapil grinding mechanics; material transformation is conceptual, not an exact recipe, ethnographic reconstruction, or universal cultural claim.`

### Runtime boundary

- authored reciprocal transforms
- bounded / instanced particles or material marks
- no full granular or soft-body solver
- no continuous physics when idle

**Selection verdict:** `BUILD_AUTHORIZED`
