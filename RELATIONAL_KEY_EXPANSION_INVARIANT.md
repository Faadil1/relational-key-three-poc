# RELATIONAL KEY — EXPANSION INVARIANT

Date: 2026-08-29
Status: CANONICAL DESIGN LAW

# 1. PRODUCT INVARIANT

# **THE RELATIONAL PAIR REMAINS THE PRODUCT.**

Every family, experiment, expansion and future implementation must preserve the original RELATIONAL KEY proposition:

`PAIR MEMBER → RELATION → OTHER MEMBER RESPONSE`

The original signature POC remains the conceptual root:

- two distinct members exist before resolution;
- membership/identity alone does not produce the outcome;
- the relationship between members changes what becomes possible;
- the relationship must be visible, manipulable and causal;
- the result must emerge through the pair rather than through an independent result widget.

Canonical behavioral grammar:

`ENCOUNTER → APPROACH → RELATE → REGISTER / RESIST → RESPONSE → CONTINUE → EXPLAIN`

A cultural or mechanical family may use a different native grammar, but it may not erase the pair.

# 2. EXPANSION PURPOSE

Expansion exists to go beyond simple card examples and make RELATIONAL KEY feel unique, physical and memorable.

Expansion is therefore NOT:

- adding Three.js because Three.js is available;
- putting a 3D object behind a 2D interaction;
- adding particles, glow, camera movement or sound as decoration;
- replacing the pair with a hero object or cinematic scene;
- turning the experience into a generic mini-game;
- letting technology become more memorable than the relationship.

Expansion IS:

# **USING THE BEST MEDIUM TO MAKE THE RELATION ITSELF MORE EXPERIENCED.**

Possible media include:

- Three.js / WebGL spatial geometry;
- WebGPU / shaders where materially useful;
- GSAP / Anime.js for mechanism-native motion;
- Web Audio for causal sound relations;
- physics / constraints for contact, tension, balance and load paths;
- depth, lighting and materials for optical/mechanical relations;
- pointer, touch, hold, drag, rotation and pressure-like interaction models;
- responsive spatial choreography;
- real-time visual state, traces or environmental response when subordinate to the pair.

# 3. MECHANISM-NATIVE TECHNOLOGY RULE

For every proposed technology or effect, ask:

# **WHAT PART OF THE RELATION BECOMES CLEARER, MORE PHYSICAL OR MORE MEMORABLE BECAUSE OF THIS?**

If the answer is only “it looks better,” the expansion is insufficiently grounded.

Examples:

- stereoscopy may use actual controlled depth/disparity behavior because depth IS the relation;
- astrolabe may use real layered rotational geometry because relative rotation IS the relation;
- Janney may use constrained pivot/latch/load behavior because mechanical coupling IS the relation;
- Valparaíso may use a shared positional constraint because equal/opposite motion IS the relation;
- Music Box may use spatial cylinder pins, contact and tooth response because mechanical decode IS the relation;
- anamorphosis may use reflective or shader-based remapping because optical rectification IS the relation;
- Hika Ahi may use accumulated friction/heat/material response because emergence through sustained contact IS the relation.

# 4. THE PAIR MAY ENTER 3D — IT MAY NOT DISAPPEAR INTO 3D

Three.js is encouraged when it allows the two members and their relation to become more legible or embodied.

Three.js must NOT convert the experience into:

- one central 3D artifact with two labels;
- a detached museum-object viewer;
- a cinematic animation the user merely watches;
- a background scene where the original pair becomes secondary UI.

The two members can become spatial objects, surfaces, mechanisms or fields, but the user must still perceive:

`MEMBER A ↔ RELATION ↔ MEMBER B`

# 5. MEMORABILITY TEST

A strong expansion should pass all of the following:

1. **Causal** — the user can tell what they did and what the counterpart did in response.
2. **Pair-native** — the memorable moment belongs to the relationship, not to a detached effect.
3. **Mechanism-native** — the motion/material/sound/depth is specific to this family.
4. **Distinctive** — the family should not feel like another family with different labels.
5. **Embodied** — direct manipulation is preferred when historically and technically truthful.
6. **Reversible / stateful** — changing or removing the relation should visibly change the outcome where the mechanism supports it.
7. **Label-independent** — with most labels hidden, the interaction should still communicate a unique relational logic.
8. **Source-truthful** — spectacle never authorizes unsupported cultural, historical or physical claims.

# 6. EXPERIENCE DEPTH LADDER

This ladder is descriptive, not a requirement that every family reach the same visual complexity.

## E0 — RELATIONAL PROOF

The pair and causal response work.

## E1 — NATIVE MOTION

The family has a mechanism-specific interaction rather than generic card movement.

## E2 — SPATIAL / MATERIAL EMBODIMENT

Depth, geometry, material, constraint, reflection, tension, contact or environmental state makes the relation more physical.

## E3 — MULTISENSORY / MEMORABLE SIGNATURE

Sound, spatial response, temporal accumulation, camera/depth, physics or another native layer creates a memorable moment that could not be swapped into another family unchanged.

A family does NOT fail merely because E2/E3 is unnecessary. The correct goal is the highest justified level, not maximum effects.

# 7. EXPANSION REVIEW RULE

Every future expansion or polish proposal must explicitly document:

- `BASE RELATION PRESERVED:` yes/no + exact chain;
- `NATIVE MECHANISM:` what is being exposed;
- `TECHNOLOGY CHOICE:` why Three.js / audio / physics / shader / 2D is appropriate;
- `MEMORABLE MOMENT:` what the user should remember;
- `PAIR VISIBILITY:` how both members remain primary;
- `REVERSAL / OTHER STATE:` how the relation changes without invalidating either member where applicable;
- `TRUTH BOUNDARY:` what is editorial vs historically/physically sourced;
- `FALLBACK:` how the relational proof survives if advanced rendering/audio is unavailable.

# 8. REGRESSION CONSEQUENCE

B2.5 and every later full-collection gate must verify two separate questions:

## A. RELATIONAL INTEGRITY — BLOCKING

Does the family still satisfy the original RELATIONAL KEY idea?

If NO: regression FAIL.

## B. EXPERIENTIAL AMBITION — DIAGNOSTIC / FUTURE REFINEMENT INPUT

Is the family using its native mechanism to create a sufficiently unique and memorable experience?

A weak answer does not retroactively invalidate a previously proven family during B2.5, but it creates explicit `EXPERIENCE DEBT` for the next refinement phase.

This prevents regression from becoming uncontrolled redesign while ensuring expansion never settles into a collection of simple skinned cases.

# 9. FINAL LAW

# **EXPANSION MUST DEEPEN THE RELATION, NOT REPLACE IT.**

# **TECHNOLOGY SERVES THE RELATIONAL MECHANISM.**

# **THE MEMORABLE MOMENT MUST BELONG TO THE PAIR.**
