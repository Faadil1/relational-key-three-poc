## Why this pass exists
The first live user video confirmed the two-card architecture but exposed a material-mechanism failure: the successful relationship was represented mainly by floating rhombi and a central register, while the actual card-edge tile material remained mostly static.

This pass keeps the composition and real-source material, but moves the causal event to the card edges.

## Corrective law
**THE EDGE MATERIAL RESOLVES THE RELATIONSHIP.**

The register is now subordinate. The visible proof must come from cut pieces that originate at Card A and Card B, rotate toward complementary fit, and close the grout/tessellation channel.

## Interaction
`CUT → APPROACH → ROTATE → FIT → REGISTER → TESSELLATE → CONTINUE`

Matching:
- two cards remain visible from frame one,
- cut pieces protrude from the inner edges,
- APPROACH brings the cards closer,
- FIT rotates edge pieces toward zero residual,
- REGISTER reduces the central datum,
- TESSELLATE fills the gap with material-derived pieces,
- completed edge band remains as the proof state.

No-match:
- pieces attempt fit,
- angular/spacing residual remains visible,
- grout channel does not close,
- restrained recoil,
- both credentials remain valid.
