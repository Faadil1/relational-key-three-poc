## SYSTEM A — FIVE-YEAR SPINE

### Thesis
The credential carries a five-stage longitudinal spine representing the full secondary-school journey.

### Translation
- Registered Continuation path becomes a five-segment civic spine.
- Each segment corresponds to one school year, but is graphic structure only unless a future system explicitly supports state updates.
- Current issue year may be indicated by print/issuance metadata, not simulated usage history.
- The junction remains the only live interaction proof.

### Why it fits STO
The same card is retained through the entire secondary-school period.

### Risk
Can become a timeline gimmick if the five stages are too literal.

## SYSTEM C — ZERO FARE / REAL PASSAGE

### Thesis
A $0 entitlement still leaves a real registration event.

### Translation
- Value is represented by a completed registration seam, not by price.
- Before tap: interrupted line.
- At validator: seam resolves.
- After tap: same path continues.
- Small printed language can distinguish entitlement from transaction: PASSAGE REGISTERED / FARE $0.
- No fake usage counter or stored trip history.

### Why it fits STO
Multi Jeunesse must be validated even when the fare is $0.

### Risk
Closer to core RELATIONAL KEY behavior than to a visibly new edition; best used as behavioral layer rather than entire visual identity.

### Score
Evidence 10 / Distinctiveness 9 / Relational Fit 10 / Specificity 9 / Manufacturability 10 / Series Contribution 7 = 55/60

Verdict: PROMOTE AS BEHAVIOR LAYER
