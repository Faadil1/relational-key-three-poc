Status: PARTIAL PASS / PROVENANCE HOLD

## Goal
Build a source-grounded visual corpus for the STO Youth Civic Mobility pilot before producing a Cultural Edition. Separate verified public evidence from design inference.

## Verified public evidence

### 1. 2024 Multi Jeunesse launch
Official STO launch text describes a new permanent card with a "look vivant" featuring "icônes jeunesses et régionales". The card is photo-less, tied to the student's school profile, valid for five years, and can carry a monthly pass or e-wallet.
Source: https://www.sto.ca/actualites/carte-multi-jeunesse-une-mobilite-durable-pour-tous-les-eleves-des-ecoles-secondaires-de-gatineau-a-lannee/

### 2. 2025-2026 Multi Jeunesse calendar
The official calendar contains a colorful Multi Jeunesse card graphic and a reminder to keep the card through the end of secondary school. Machine-readable text embedded around the card graphic includes local place/road labels such as GATINEAU, Saint-Joseph and Boulevard Maloney O. This is evidence that the youth visual ecosystem references local geography, but it is not enough to identify the exact icon set or its designer.
Source: https://sto.ca/site/assets/files/4837/calendrier-multi-jeunesse_2025-2026.pdf

### 3. 2022-2023 evolution
The youth free-access program starts in 2022 and is renewed in 2023. 2023 official wording refers to a Multi card "aux couleurs du libre-accès". 2024 is the clear transition to a durable five-year Multi Jeunesse object rather than a summer-only campaign layer.
Sources:
- https://www.sto.ca/actualites/retour-du-libre-acces-jeunesse-a-gatineau-les-etudiants-de-niveau-secondaire-invites-a-prendre-dassaut-le-reseau/
- https://www.sto.ca/actualites/deux-promotions-prolongees-afin-de-faciliter-les-deplacements-sur-le-reseau-de-transport-en-commun-a-gatineau/

### 4. Youth mobility communication ecosystem
Ado Gatineau frames youth transport around autonomy, mixed-mode movement and self-directed mobility (walking, cycling, bus). The STO is presented as part of that autonomy ecosystem.
Source: https://www.gatineau.ca/portail/default.aspx?c=en-CA&p=ado_gatineau%2Ftransport

MOBI-O also produced a dedicated Multi Jeunesse mobility capsule centered on young Gatineau residents using public transit in everyday life, with economic, ecological and social benefits.
Source: https://mobi-o.ca/campagnes/capsules-mobilite-durable/

## What is visually/provenance verified
- A distinct 2024 Multi Jeunesse visual identity exists.
- STO itself describes it as colorful/lively and using youth + regional icons.
- The 2025-2026 official calendar reuses that visual identity.
- Local geographic language/labels appear in the visual ecosystem.
- Youth communication around the card emphasizes autonomy, social life, mixed mobility and repeated use.

## What is NOT yet verified
- Exact inventory of all icons on the card.
- Exact meaning of each icon.
- Whether icons were custom-drawn, licensed, adapted or sourced from an existing library.
- Designer / agency / illustrator attribution.
- Exact 2022 and 2023 card artwork at sufficient resolution for formal visual comparison.
- Whether any 2024 icon is intended as an official symbol of a specific Gatineau landmark.

Therefore we must not claim icon provenance or copy specific unexplained iconography.

## Visual mechanisms promoted for design exploration

### A. LOCAL GEOGRAPHY AS IDENTITY — PROMOTE
Locality can be encoded through routes, road names, districts, transfer points, or spatial fragments rather than generic regional symbols.

### B. LONGITUDINAL OBJECT — PROMOTE
The same physical credential accompanies the student through five years. The design can behave like a persistent civic companion rather than a disposable campaign card.

### C. FREE DOES NOT MEAN INVISIBLE — PROMOTE
Even at $0 fare, the card must be validated. This maps strongly to Registered Continuation: benefit exists only after the relationship is registered.

### D. COMMON OBJECT / PERSONAL MARK — PROMOTE
All students receive the same object but add their own name. This supports a common civic shell with a personal trace layer.

### E. AUTONOMY ECOSYSTEM — PROMOTE
The strongest youth framing is not "school" but movement toward independence: friends, work, activities, mixed transport and self-planned routes.

### F. ICON COLLAGE AS STYLE — HOLD
STO confirms youth/regional icons exist, but their exact provenance is unresolved. We should not simply copy the icon collage as a skin.

## Current strongest concept territory

### FIVE-YEAR CIVIC COMPANION
A youth credential that visually records the transition from school-bound mobility to independent city movement while preserving the same relational geometry.

Supporting mechanics:
- common civic object
- personal mark
- repeated validation
- local geography
- persistent five-year object
- network-bound entitlement
