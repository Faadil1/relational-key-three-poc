## Product invariant
**THE RELATIONAL PAIR REMAINS THE PRODUCT.**

## Pair roles
- Card A — LEFT VIEW
- Card B — RIGHT VIEW
- Gap — BINOCULAR FUSION / DEPTH FIELD

## Signature interaction
`OFFSET → APPROACH → PAIR → FUSE → DEPTH → REGISTER → CONTINUE`

## Matching relationship
Both cards retain distinct views of the same stereoscopic subject. During registration the fusion field reduces gross separation but preserves a small relational disparity. The proof state is not equality: it is a stable depth cue generated by the pair.

After the first live audit, the runtime was patched so `FUSE`, `DEPTH`, and `REGISTER` are separate visual phases rather than a single registered state.

During `FUSE`, left/right ghosting stabilizes while the views remain distinct.

During `DEPTH`, three subtle editorial bands composited from both source views separate by only a few pixels and small scale differences. The photographic material therefore carries more of the depth cue. The fusion field briefly enlarges, then settles at `REGISTER`.

The older rectangular depth planes remain only as a transitional search cue and fade strongly once the photographic depth cue appears.

## Other relationship
The second state keeps a larger visible viewpoint residual. The system does not mark either card invalid; it simply does not promote a single selected depth cue for this pair.

This path was intentionally preserved during the visual patch.

## Visual material
Public-domain London stereocard, International Exhibition, 1871. The source image already contains a historical left/right stereoscopic pair; Card A and Card B deliberately use opposite halves.

## Editorial translation
The central layered parallax, planes, and fusion dot are interface abstractions. They are not a recovered historical depth map and do not claim exact stereoscopic calibration.

## Distinctive system contribution
**CONTROLLED DISPARITY PRODUCES DEPTH.**

It extends the system beyond zero-residual registration while remaining distinct from Ombak: Ombak proves relation through temporal acoustic beating; Binocular Register proves relation through spatial viewpoint disparity and perceptual fusion.
