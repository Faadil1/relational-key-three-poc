# ROUND 12C.1 — BENIN + NIGERIA COMMUNICATION ARCHIVE PILOTS

## Purpose

Test whether RELATIONAL KEY Cultural Editions can survive a radical domain shift from civic mobility into historic communication infrastructure.

This is **not** a combined West Africa visual identity. Benin and Nigeria remain separate cultural/archival studies that share a communication-domain research family.

---

## Evidence boundary

### Tier A / institutional context

**Benin**
- Archives nationales du Bénin: Série J — Télécommunications covers posts, telephone links, transmissions, and the organization/functioning of posts and telecommunications services.
- ARCEP Bénin provides institutional history/context for telecommunications access, SIM evolution, numbering, tariffs, and electronic communications.

**Nigeria**
- National Archives of Nigeria preserves documentary collections spanning political, social and cultural history, including photographs, maps and other archival records.

### Tier B / object evidence

Historic phonecard objects are still primarily documented through collector/marketplace scans and community memory rather than first-party museum catalogues.

Therefore:
- valid for mechanism discovery,
- useful for object anatomy and recurring motifs,
- not sufficient by itself for authoritative national design-history claims.

# BENIN PILOT — SERVICE AS STORYBOARD

## Archive signals

Recurring historic card/object labels include:
- Conférence à Trois
- Services Plus
- Internet
- Telephone Tariffs
- Francophonie summit editions
- service instructions / tariff grids

## Mechanism extracted

**SERVICE EXPLANATION IS THE GRAPHIC SUBJECT.**

The card is not only a stored-value object. It can explain what a communications service does, how it is activated, or how access is structured.

## RELATIONAL KEY translation

### Working name
**SERVICE REGISTER**

### Law
> A relationship becomes legible when the service path explains itself as it registers.

### Geometry
The Registered Continuation path is segmented into functional service stages rather than geographic fragments.

Possible primitive sequence:

`CLAIM → SERVICE STEP → REGISTRATION → SERVICE STEP → CONNECTION`

The path may carry tiny service annotations, numbered stages or state transitions.

### What must NOT happen
- no generic African patterning,
- no literal copying of vintage phonecard graphics,
- no flag palette shortcut,
- no decorative telephone icon collage.

# NIGERIA PILOT — INFRASTRUCTURE AS PORTRAIT

## Archive signals

Recurring historic object families include:
- NITEL Earth Station cards in multiple denominations,
- NITEL arrow denomination cards,
- AUTEL TESTCARD / sample language,
- TELIPOINT cards,
- PCNL cardphone-launch cards,
- satellite / earth-station imagery.

## Mechanism extracted

**INFRASTRUCTURE ITSELF CAN BECOME IDENTITY.**

The communication system is not hidden behind branding. Earth stations, signal systems, cardphone launches, denomination families and test/prototype language become the subject.

## RELATIONAL KEY translation

### Working name
**SIGNAL PORTRAIT**

### Law
> Access becomes visible when a credential registers with the infrastructure capable of carrying its signal.

### Geometry
The destination counterpart becomes an infrastructural field rather than a city field.

Possible primitives:
- radial signal arcs,
- ground-station node,
- relay alignment,
- denomination bands,
- test / registered states,
- one dominant infrastructure emblem generated from geometry rather than illustration.

### What must NOT happen
- no generic satellite sci-fi aesthetic,
- no neon radar UI,
- no literal NITEL logo recreation,
- no flattening Nigeria into telecom nostalgia.
